What it does

Powerful features

Everything the campus team needs to monitor, understand, and act on building performance.

  • Interactive Energy Dashboards

    Real-time visual dashboards for energy consumption, solar generation, and system performance, powered by Power BI Embedded.

  • AI-Powered Data Chat

    Ask plain-English questions about your facility's data and get instant, accurate answers, no SQL required.

  • Automated Energy Forecasting

    Predictive charts project upcoming energy use using historical sensor data, so you can plan ahead with confidence.

  • Emissions Tracking Built In

    Automatically calculates CO2 emissions using the local grid emission factor, turning raw energy data into environmental impact.

  • Weather-Correlated Insights

    Compares outdoor weather conditions against building performance to reveal how temperature and humidity affect energy use.

  • Four-Tier Secure Access

    Every user signs in through Microsoft, with SuperAdmin, Admin, Staff, and Viewer roles controlling exactly what they can see and do.
